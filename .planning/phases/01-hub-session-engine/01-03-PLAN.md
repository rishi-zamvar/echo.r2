## Phase Goal

**As a** solo operator of the home PC hub, **I want to** start Claude Code sessions in the hub and watch, steer, interrupt, and stop them from a client that can drop and reconnect, **so that** sessions keep working without me and I always know their live state.

<objective>
Steer-and-stop slice: Rishi can send text, interrupt, and stop a session from the CLI; writes from many clients are serialized through one queue; external sessions are read-only; stop and boot sweep kill whole process trees.

Purpose: SESS-04 (interrupt / stop kills tree) and SESS-05 (one writer, externals read-only).
Output: write queue + killTree + sweep in sessions.js; stop route + interrupt frame in server.js; send/interrupt/stop in cli.js.
</objective>
